Our Story

Islamabad's Premier Steakhouse Experience

El Momento is the best steakhouse in Islamabad that offers quality steaks in a great environment. Nestled at Beverly Centre on Jinnah Avenue, we've created a sanctuary for those who live for great food.

We serve various steaks, including premium imported cuts such as Rib-Eye, Filet Mignon, and T-Bone — aged to perfection and prepared by passionate chefs who treat every plate as a masterpiece.
